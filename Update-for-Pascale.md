- **Told Google exactly what you offer** — that this is a psychosocial counselling, supervision and coaching practice in **Bern**, run by you, with your qualifications (SGFB / BSO). This makes it far more likely to appear when someone searches for those services in Bern.

- **Added your logo as the little icon** you see in the browser tab and in bookmarks.

- **Kept out the pages that shouldn't appear in Google** (like the "thank you" and error pages).

I did **not** change any of your written content — the words are exactly as you provided them.

## 3. One small thing I noticed — your name appears two ways

While working on the site, I noticed your name is written in two forms:

- **Pascale Myriam Kohli** — used across the main pages (Home, Beratung, Supervision, Über mich, etc.)
- **Pascale Yska Kohli** — used on the legal pages (AGB, Datenschutz, Impressum)

My understanding is that **Myriam is your professional/public name** and **Yska is your legal name** — which is completely normal, and using the legal name on legal pages is correct.

**Why it matters:** search engines and potential clients associate a practice with **one consistent name**. When two versions appear, it can slightly:

- **split your recognition** — Google is less certain the two names are the same person, which can weaken your search presence;
- **look less polished** to a first-time visitor.

**What I've done for now:** I've used **Pascale Myriam Kohli** consistently everywhere public and in the search-engine information, and kept **Pascale Yska Kohli** only on the legal pages, where the legal name belongs. I also corrected one spot where the two had been mixed by accident.

👉 **Could you confirm this is right?** If you'd prefer a different arrangement — for example, one single name everywhere — just let me know and I'll adjust it.
